## Limits

- curb_stall: Ohio and Georgia pauses are campaign pledges (Fortune, 4 Nov 2026).
- tceq_permits: Registry dates only. Pending counts move as TCEQ updates records.
- eia_slip: Grid-reported plants. On-site data-centre plants mostly sit outside EIA-860M.
- turbine_imports: Counts units, of any make, landing in the US.
- texas_delay: Delay counts from 31 Dec of the year EIP lists, the shortest possible delay.
